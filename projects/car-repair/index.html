FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking guarantees your slot and shortens your wait. Emergencies are handled around the clock.

How long do repairs usually take?

Most common repairs are completed the same day. For bigger jobs we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — all repairs are backed by a 24-month parts and labour warranty.

Will you call before doing extra work?

Always. We send an itemised quote with photos and only proceed once you approve it.
