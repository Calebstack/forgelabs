About Our Shop

Your Trusted Dealership
Alternative.

At AutoFix Pro, we believe in honest work, transparent pricing, and treating every vehicle as if it were our own. Founded in 2011, our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and manned by certified master technicians.

Whether you need routine maintenance or a complex engine rebuild, we deliver dealership-quality service without the dealership price tag. We take pride in educating our customers so you can make informed decisions about your vehicle's health.

  • Free comprehensive digital vehicle inspections with photos.
  • Comfortable waiting area with free Wi-Fi and premium coffee.
  • Complimentary shuttle service and loaner vehicles available.

Comprehensive Auto Services

Engine Diagnostics

Check engine light on? Our advanced computer diagnostics pinpoint issues quickly and accurately to save you time and money.

Brake Repair
