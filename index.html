FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of projects do you take on?
Websites, web applications, UI/UX design and branding for small and growing businesses — plus custom software when you need something more specific.
How long does a typical website take?
A simple website takes about 2 to 3 days, while a larger one takes around 4 to 5 days. We'll agree on a clear timeline with you before we start.
Will my website work on phones?
Yes. Everything we build is responsive and tested on mobile, tablet and desktop, with light and dark-friendly design where it makes sense.
